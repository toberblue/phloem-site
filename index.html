Phloem

A document that remembers how it was made.

What it is

Phloem is a writing environment built on one premise: the record of how a piece of writing came to be is worth keeping, and it belongs to the writer. Every act on the page — typed, pasted, accepted from the machine, informed by a suggestion but rewritten in your own hand — lands as an event in a sealed, append-only log. The log is the document.

Three things it does differently

Help that waits to be claimed

AI assistance lives inside the document, in the margin, as offers. Nothing enters the page except by the writer's own gesture — and when it does, it is credited as what it is.

The middle band, made visible

Between copying and pure invention sits the way people actually learn: read it, understand it, write it back in your own words. Because Phloem knows what it showed you, it can attest that path with evidence — no other tool can.

A record you own

The authorship report is the writer's, exported and shared by consent. It is a spectrum, not a verdict. Witness — not police.
